FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

Is it necessary to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

No, basic URL shortening is available without an account. However, creating a free account unlocks custom links, QR code generation, and analytics tracking.

Are the shortened links permanent? Will they expire?

Shortened links created with Scissor are permanent and will not expire as long as they comply with our terms of service.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

Free accounts can shorten an unlimited number of basic URLs. Advanced features like custom domain links and bulk generation vary by plan.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Yes! You can customize your URL slugs (e.g., scissor.ly/my-brand) and even connect your custom branded domains.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Yes, Scissor provides detailed analytics including total clicks, geographic location, referrers, and device types.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are the shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

We use real-time security scanning and encrypted SSL routing to protect users against malware, phishing, and spammy links.

What is a QR code and what can it do?

A QR code is a two-dimensional scannable barcode that instantly directs smartphone cameras to your shortened URL, digital menu, or business profile.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my own applications or websites?

Yes, our robust REST API lets you shorten URLs, generate QR codes, and fetch analytics programmatically from your own applications.
